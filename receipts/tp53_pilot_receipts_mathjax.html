Methods: Key Equations

$$\mathrm{AUROC} = \mathbb{P}\left( s^+ > s^- \right)$$
AUROC as concordance probability.
$$\mathrm{AUROC} = \int_0^1 \mathrm{TPR}(\mathrm{FPR})\, d\,\mathrm{FPR}$$
AUROC as area under ROC.
$$\mathrm{AP} = \sum_k P(k)\,\Delta R(k)$$
Average Precision (area under Precision–Recall).
$$J(t) = \mathrm{TPR}(t) - \mathrm{FPR}(t), \quad t^\star = \arg\max_t J(t)$$
Youden’s J and the optimal threshold.
$$\mathrm{NB}_{\mathrm{model}}(p_t) = \frac{\mathrm{TP}}N - \frac{p_t}{1-p_t}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{FP}}N$$
Decision Curve Analysis: model net benefit at threshold $p_t$.
$$\mathrm{NB}_{\mathrm{all}}(p_t) = \mathrm{prev} - \frac{p_t}{1-p_t}\,(1-\mathrm{prev})$$
Net benefit for treat-all.
$$\mathrm{NB}_{\mathrm{none}} = 0$$
Net benefit for treat-none.
$$\mathrm{ECE} = \sum_{b=1}^B \frac{n_b}N \left|\mathrm{acc}_b - \mathrm{conf}_b\right|$$
Expected Calibration Error over $B$ bins.
$$W^+ = \sum_{i: d_i>0} \mathrm{rank}\!\left(|d_i|\right), \quad d_i=x_i-y_i$$
Wilcoxon signed-rank (one-sided) on per-fold deltas.
